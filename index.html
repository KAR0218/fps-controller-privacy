Privacy Policy for FPS Controller

Effective date: September 29, 2026

FPS Controller ("the App") is an Android application developed and published under the developer name FPS Controller. This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, and handles information when you use FPS Controller.

1. Information the App Accesses

FPS Controller may access information about applications installed on your Android device. This includes application package names and information needed to display installed applications in the App.

The App uses Android's QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission for this purpose. This access is required so that FPS Controller can display installed applications and allow you to select an application for which you want to apply or reset Android Game Mode FPS settings.

The installed-application information is used locally on your device for the functionality of FPS Controller. Based on the current version of the App, this information is not transmitted by FPS Controller to the developer's servers or used for advertising.

2. How the Information Is Used

Information about installed applications is used only to:

  • display applications available on your device;
  • allow you to select an application;
  • apply supported Android Game Mode FPS settings to the selected application;
  • change or reset those settings when requested by you.

FPS Controller does not use installed-application information to build advertising profiles or to sell information to third parties.

3. Shizuku

FPS Controller uses the Shizuku framework to obtain the permissions required to perform supported Android system operations on your behalf.

Shizuku is a separate Android service. FPS Controller uses its API to perform the supported operations required by the App. FPS Controller does not use Shizuku to modify game files, inject code into applications, modify APK files, or intercept network traffic.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

FPS Controller does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, address, telephone number, email address, or other personal information in order to use the App.

The App does not contain advertising and does not sell personal information.

Based on the current version of the App, FPS Controller does not transmit the installed-application information described above to the developer or to an external server.

FPS Controller does not intentionally share user information with advertising networks, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or profiling purposes.

5. Data Storage and Retention

FPS Controller does not maintain a developer-operated server account or remote database containing your installed-application information. Information accessed by the App for its functionality is handled locally on the Android device.

If you uninstall FPS Controller, information stored by the App as part of its normal Android application data is removed according to Android's standard application data handling.

6. Security

FPS Controller is designed to process the information required for its functionality locally on the device and to avoid unnecessary transmission of such information.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should keep their Android device and operating system appropriately protected.

7. Children's Privacy

FPS Controller is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

FPS Controller uses the Shizuku framework to perform certain Android system operations. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the applicable information provided by those services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality, data handling practices, or applicable legal or platform requirements change. Any updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of FPS Controller, you can contact the developer through the developer contact information provided on the FPS Controller listing on Google Play.
